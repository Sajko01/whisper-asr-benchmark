 Artificial intelligence is a field of computer science focused on developing systems capable of solving problems, making decisions and understanding human speech. Automatic speech recognition enables the conversion of audio signals into text and is widely used in modern applications. Deep learning-based models have significantly improved the quality and speed of speech processing. The Whisper model developed by OpenIUse's neural networks trained on large data sets to enable speech recognition across many languages. Faster Whisper is an optimized implementation of the Whisper model based on the C-translate to library. The goal of this experiment is to analyze execution time and compare the performance of different speech recognition system implementations. Industrial intelligence continues to play an important role in speech technology, machine learning and natural language processing researchers constantly develop new methods to improve accuracy, efficiency and scalability of speech recognition systems.
